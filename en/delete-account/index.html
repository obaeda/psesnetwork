Delete account and data

You can start account deletion even if the app was removed or you cannot sign in.

Fastest: inside the app

  1. Open Account, then Settings.
  2. Open Privacy and device.
  3. Select Delete account and data and complete identity confirmation.

Outside the app

Send the request from the email address associated with your account and include the account name or phone number so ownership can be verified. Never send a password or verification code.

Send deletion request

Email: psespalestine@gmail.com

Before deletion:
  • An active order may need to be cancelled when eligible or completed first.
  • After account ownership is verified, the account and associated data are deleted from active systems.
  • Limited records may remain where necessary for security, fraud prevention, disputes or legal obligations.
  • Deletion is final and the account or content may not be recoverable afterward.
