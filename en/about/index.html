Why build PSES?

A local market that understands people,
not a copy of someone else’s.

PSES is building a modern Palestinian shopping and discovery experience, grounded in local needs and using AI only where it adds real value.

Vision

The smartest Palestinian market, with trust proven in practice.

Our goal is to become the smartest and most trusted Palestinian marketplace for both experience and product accuracy. Progress means the image matches the item, choices remain attached, information stays clear, and problems have a resolution path.

Local
Arabic-first, ILS pricing, regions and delivery shaped around reality.
Smart
Analysis and suggestions that reduce uncertainty without inventing facts.
Trustworthy
Product review, structured data and server-trusted operations.
Accountable
Reporting, support, order records and clear resolution policies.

Principles

How decisions are made

01

Quality before speed

An experimental feature stays experimental until its accuracy and stability are demonstrated.

02

Facts before persuasion

Sales goals never justify invented prices, stock or AI outcomes.

03

Systems outlive individuals

Documented paths and decisions let the platform continue when people change.

04

Least necessary access

Customer, Work and Core apps have distinct roles; sensitive data is not exposed without need.

05

Clear privacy

We explain what is processed and why, with account deletion inside and outside the app.

06

Continuous improvement

Real-device checks, tests and critique are part of development, not a final ceremony.
